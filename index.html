Before She Came Home

Am I her?

Your own parents are hunting you. A first-person stealth horror game set in Arizona, 1980.

Play the free demo Get notified

Coming to Steam in 2027 · Windows PC · English and Russian

“Third. You’re the third in seven years.”

Arizona, 1980. Seven years after she vanished, Sara Brown knocks on the door of the house she remembers. Her father answers with a shotgun. Two girls have already come to this door claiming to be his daughter, and he sends the third one away. Sara isn’t sure he’s wrong.

That night, in the storm, she breaks back in to find out. By morning, she’ll have to get out alive.

  • Joseph hunts. Olivia listens. Buck barks. Joseph patrols the yard and the house with a flashlight and a shotgun. Olivia hears every creak; she won’t come after you herself, she’ll call Joseph. And Buck, chained in the yard, barks at anything that moves.
  • Three strikes. The first time Joseph catches you, he throws you out past the gate and takes what you found. The second time, he locks you in the barn. The third time, it’s over. (Easy mode gives you checkpoints instead.)
  • You can’t fight. You can’t hide forever. Hide in wardrobes, under beds, in the hay, or throw a bottle to lure them away. But stay hidden too long and Sara starts to panic, and her sobbing carries.
  • Learn the house, not the route. The crowbar, the meat for the dog and most of the notes turn up in different places every playthrough.
  • Some doors open onto 1973. At the gate, the storm falls away and it’s a summer afternoon: laundry on the line, her mother calling her in for supper. On the stairs, there’s a piano that isn’t there anymore.
  • They wrote it all down. The notes left around the house reveal what this family has become. The basement safe opens with a date only they would remember.

About AI in this game

Character voices and the narrator are generated with AI text-to-speech (ElevenLabs) in English and Russian. The story, notes and dialogue, the English translation, the store page and website text and the trailer narration were written by the developer together with an AI writing assistant (Claude by Anthropic), then reviewed and edited by the developer. Some sound effects were generated with AI sound generation (ElevenLabs) and then edited: panic breathing, the threat and chase loops, a capture sting, a few forest and stream ambiences, Olivia’s humming, and sounds in the end-of-demo teaser, including a whistled melody. Our trailers use the same AI voices and sounds. Trailer music comes from licensed sound libraries, except a music-box lullaby we synthesized ourselves from the melody of that AI-generated whistle. Nothing is generated while you play.
